RESEARCH AT THE INTERSECTION OF
THE RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

A capable intelligent system is only useful when its actions are understandable, testable, and appropriate to the people who rely on it.

My work ranges from machine learning and graph-based methods to cyber-physical architectures and human–AI interaction. It is motivated by real questions: how to handle uncertain information, coordinate scarce resources, plan in changing environments, and preserve human judgment.

These areas overlap across emergency medicine, firefighting, autonomous vehicles, urban operations, and first-responder communications. I use computational methods, system models, and applied research to examine what technology can—and cannot yet—reliably do.
